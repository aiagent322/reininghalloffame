About Duane Latimer

Canadian horseman Duane Latimer has influenced reining across North America and Europe as a competitor and mentor. An NRHA Futurity champion, AQHA world champion and World Equestrian Games medalist, he has helped develop riders and professionals while strengthening the sport’s international reach.

Recognition & Highlights

  • 2026 Dale Wilkinson Lifetime Achievement Award recipient.
  • NRHA Two Million Dollar Rider at the August 2026 announcement.
  • Recognized for horsemanship, mentorship and international impact.
