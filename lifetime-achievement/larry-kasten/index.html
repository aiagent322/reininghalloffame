Larry Kasten

Dale Wilkinson Lifetime Achievement Award
Lifetime Achievement · 2025

About Larry Kasten

Larry Kasten’s career brings together competition, judging and education. After teaching at West Texas State University, he joined the University of Wisconsin–River Falls in 1973. His horses and students have achieved national success, reflecting a lasting commitment to horsemanship and mentorship.

Recognition & Highlights

  • 2025 Dale Wilkinson Lifetime Achievement Award recipient.
  • NRHA Futurity finalist and Super Stakes Limited Open winner.
  • AQHA and NRHA judge and longtime educator.
