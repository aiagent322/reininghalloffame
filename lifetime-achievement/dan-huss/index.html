Dan Huss

Dale Wilkinson Lifetime Achievement Award
Lifetime Achievement · 2026

Announced for 2026. Formal recognition is scheduled for December 2, 2026, at Sliders’ Night Out presented by Toyon Ranch in Oklahoma City.

About Dan Huss

Dan Huss has spent more than four decades developing horses and riders. A student of Dale Wilkinson at Findlay College, he later spent 19 years on its faculty. His accomplishments include the 2018 World Equestrian Games team gold and individual silver medals, alongside a lasting reputation as a teacher.

Recognition & Highlights

  • 2026 Dale Wilkinson Lifetime Achievement Award recipient.
  • 1987 NRHA Futurity Limited Open champion.
  • His bridleless 2018 AQHA World Show run followed an equipment malfunction.
