Cheryl Cody

NRHA Hall of Fame
Person / organization · 2025

About Cheryl Cody

A writer, photographer and event professional, Cheryl Cody has served the reining community for more than four decades. The lifetime NRHA member co-founded Rein In Cancer and leads Pro Management Inc., supporting major reining events and programs for exhibitors and young people.

Recognition & Highlights

  • NRHA Hall of Fame, Class of 2025.
  • Co-founder and president of Rein In Cancer.
  • President of Pro Management Inc.
