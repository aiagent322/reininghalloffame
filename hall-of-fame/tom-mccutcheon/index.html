Tom McCutcheon

NRHA Hall of Fame
Person / organization · 2026

Announced for 2026. Formal recognition is scheduled for December 2, 2026, at Sliders’ Night Out presented by Toyon Ranch in Oklahoma City.

About Tom McCutcheon

Tom McCutcheon has helped shape reining as a competitor, breeder, mentor and industry organizer. His contributions include helping establish the National Reining Breeders Classic and Legacy Sale, serving NRHA in leadership roles, and supporting development of its Foal Nomination Program.

Recognition & Highlights

  • Selected for the NRHA Hall of Fame Class of 2026.
  • World Equestrian Games team gold medalist and individual world champion.
  • NRHA Two Million Dollar Rider and Two Million Dollar Owner at the August 2026 announcement.
