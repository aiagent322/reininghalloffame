Mike Hancock

NRHA Hall of Fame
Person / organization · 2026

Announced for 2026. Formal recognition is scheduled for December 2, 2026, at Sliders’ Night Out presented by Toyon Ranch in Oklahoma City.

About Mike Hancock

Mike Hancock’s contribution to reining spans competition, volunteer leadership and philanthropy. A two-term NRHA president, he has supported youth development, scholarships and the Reining Horse Foundation while helping guide the association through periods of change and growth.

Recognition & Highlights

  • Selected for the NRHA Hall of Fame Class of 2026.
  • Two-term NRHA president.
  • Reining Horse Foundation Double Platinum Lifetime Donor.
